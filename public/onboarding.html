Welcome aboard.

Here's how to get your broadcast up and running in four steps.

1
Set up your team
Head to Setup to name your event, build your athlete roster, and configure your boats and lineups. Everything the overlay uses comes from here.
2
Add the overlay to OBS
In OBS, add a Browser Source and paste in your overlay URL. Set the resolution to match your stream (1920×1080 recommended).
https://your-app.railway.app/overlay.html
3
Use the Deck to go live Live
Open /deck.html on any device during your race. Use it to start timers, trigger animated intros, and display results — all in real time without touching OBS.
4
You're ready
Hit the button below to open the broadcast control panel and start your first session. You can revisit Setup and Deck any time from the navigation.
